# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Brazilian voters researching candidates on a phone, often with limited time
and attention, who need to compare public information and prepare a reliable
printed voting note before entering the polling booth.

## Product Purpose

Colinha Eleitoral helps voters research candidates for the 2026 General
Elections, understand the financial and judicial information published by
official sources, and print a compact candidate list for election day.

## Brand Personality

Clear, trustworthy, and serene. The interface should reduce uncertainty without
creating alarm, explain the origin and limits of public data, and use plain
language for consequential information.

## Anti-references

Avoid dense government-portal layouts, generic document lists with repeated
labels, alarmist language about judicial records, and interfaces that make
users scroll through long unstructured lists. Avoid hiding important context
behind ambiguous icons or relying on color alone to communicate status.

## Design Principles

- Make official-source information understandable at a glance.
- Group related evidence before showing individual documents.
- Preserve context: source, document type, date, and limitations should travel
  with the result.
- Use progressive disclosure to keep the primary voter workflow calm.
- Make the printed output more useful than the screen it came from.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for interactive surfaces. Maintain visible keyboard focus,
semantic controls, readable contrast, touch targets of at least 44px, and
status communication that does not depend on color alone.

Assume many voters only use a phone for calls, WhatsApp, photos, and simple
browsing. Prefer list-first choice over typing, plain Portuguese over jargon,
large type, one clear next step, and never auto-trigger print or system dialogs.
